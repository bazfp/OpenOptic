Export

Raw TIFF preserves all received RGB samples without host processing. Aligned/processed TIFF saves the working image. Neither TIFF applies preview inversion, contrast, gamma or rotation. Capture-mode raw data already includes hardware shading. In custom multi-pass scans, raw export saves the first normal-exposure RGB pass.
